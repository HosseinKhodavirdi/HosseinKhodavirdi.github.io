High-power transducer development for wafer processing

Piezoelectric devices · Acoustics · System engineering

Developing a novel high-power piezoelectric transducer and acoustic system for semiconductor wafer processing. The work spans device architecture, piezoelectric and structural simulation, resonance and impedance analysis, vibration measurements, and system-level drive and matching considerations. Experimental characterization is used to assess model predictions and guide design iterations.

Photoacoustics and ultrasound signal analysis

University of Illinois Chicago

Worked on computational and experimental methods for photoacoustic microscopy and ultrasound. Developed Python and MATLAB workflows for multispectral signal analysis, spectral unmixing, waveform decomposition, and quantitative image analysis. Compared decomposition methods and modeled thermoacoustic and photoacoustic signal generation against ultrasound measurements.
