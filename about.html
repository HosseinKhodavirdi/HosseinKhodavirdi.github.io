Profile

How I work

My background is in mechanical wave physics and computational mechanics, and my current work focuses on developing acoustic systems for real engineering applications. I use analytical reasoning, finite-element modeling, and careful experiments as complementary tools.

For transducer development, I am interested in the full chain from material properties and geometry to impedance, vibration, coupling, and system performance. I use model-to-test comparison to identify what a model captures, where it falls short, and which design variables deserve attention.

My previous research in photoacoustics and ultrasound broadened this perspective to include signal processing, imaging measurements, and quantitative analysis.

Technical perspective

Connected methods

Physics-based modeling

Analytical wave models, finite elements, piezoelectric coupling, harmonic response, and thermoelasticity.

Experimental characterization

Laser Doppler vibrometry, impedance measurements, oscilloscope measurements, and RF excitation.

System-level design

Equivalent circuits, lumped-element models, electrical matching, and drive-circuit considerations.

Scientific computing

Python and MATLAB workflows for simulation, signal processing, parameter studies, and automation.
