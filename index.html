01 / About

From physical insight
to practical systems.

My work connects acoustic and elastic wave physics with the design and characterization of electromechanical devices.

I work as a Senior Acoustic Engineer at Crystal Sonic in Phoenix, developing piezoelectric systems for semiconductor wafer processing. My approach combines analytical models, finite element simulations, and laboratory measurements to understand device behavior and guide engineering decisions.

My academic research background includes mechanical wave physics and computational mechanics at Illinois Institute of Technology. I am particularly interested in the connection between material properties, device geometry, electrical excitation, and mechanical response.

02 / Research & engineering

Areas of work

Connecting wave mechanics, device design, and experimental validation.

I / ELECTROMECHANICAL SYSTEMS

Piezoelectric transducers

Modeling resonance, impedance, and vibration in piezoelectric devices. Connecting material models and equivalent circuits with measured electrical and mechanical response.

Piezoelectricity · Harmonic analysis · Equivalent circuits
II / COMPUTATIONAL MECHANICS

Waves & multiphysics

Studying acoustic and elastic wave propagation and coupled material response through analytical methods and numerical simulation, including thermal stress in layered structures.

Finite elements · Wave propagation · Thermoelasticity
III / EXPERIMENTAL METHODS

Measurement & validation

Characterizing vibration and electrical impedance, processing experimental signals, and comparing measurements with numerical predictions to identify sources of disagreement.

Laser Doppler vibrometry · Impedance · Signal processing
