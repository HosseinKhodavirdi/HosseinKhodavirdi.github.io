Professional profile

From research to working systems.

I am a Senior Acoustic Engineer at Crystal Sonic, where I work on acoustic and piezoelectric systems for semiconductor wafer processing. My engineering practice combines device modeling, experimental characterization, and iterative design.

My academic work spans mechanical wave physics, computational mechanics, photoacoustics, and ultrasound. I value clear model-to-test comparisons and practical explanations of how material and design choices shape performance.
